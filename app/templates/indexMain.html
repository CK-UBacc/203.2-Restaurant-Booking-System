Features

Online Booking

Accept reservations around the clock directly from your website. No phone calls needed.

Live Table View

Special Requests

Guests can note dietary requirements, allergies, and special occasions so you're always prepared.

Email Confirmation

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance can I book?+

You can book up to {{ restaurant_settings.max_advance_days if restaurant_settings else 30 }} days in advance. We recommend booking early for weekends and special occasions.

Can I modify or cancel my booking?+

Please contact us directly by phone or email to modify or cancel your reservation. We are happy to help as long as you give us enough notice.

What is the maximum group size?+

Our online booking system accepts groups up to {{ restaurant_settings.max_party_size if restaurant_settings else 8 }} guests. For larger parties please call us directly.

Will I receive a confirmation?+

Yes. After submitting your booking you will see a confirmation page with your reference number and receive a confirmation email with all the details.
